How this works. Every minute, your browser pulls live scores, lineups and projections from Sleeper and game clocks from ESPN, then plays out the rest of the season 20,000 times. Starters still to play are drawn around their Sleeper projection, scaled by how much of their NFL game is left, with teammates moving partly together. Future games use each team's season scoring average. Odds are converted to American odds for fun, with no vig.
Not affiliated with Sleeper, ESPN or the NFL.
